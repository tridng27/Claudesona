# Song credits

These songs are compiled into Claudesona and play in her built-in playlist, "Claudesona's songs". The player shows each one as "Title · Artist".

Files here are named `Artist - Title.mp3` (`build.rs` reads the credit from the name). They were re-encoded to 128 kbps and loudness-matched (HoliznaCC0 songs to about -24 LUFS so they sit quietly under her); nothing else was changed.
